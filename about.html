Our story

Built to close the gap between business and technology.

Stacksol Technologies Private Limited was incorporated on 3rd September 2024 by directors Dhruv Sandilya and Pravesh Kumar Pathak with one clear idea: make enterprise-grade technology simple to buy, deploy and depend on.

Headquartered in New Delhi, we deliver IT solutions, hardware supply and professional services for enterprises, SMEs and government organisations — spanning endpoint computing, managed printing, cloud, cybersecurity, networking and enterprise software.

As an authorised Government e-Marketplace (GeM) seller with regular GST registration, we bring the same transparency and compliance to public-sector procurement as we do to private engagements.
