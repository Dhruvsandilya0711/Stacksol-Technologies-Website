About Stacksol Technologies

Empowering businesses with innovative IT solutions, reliable hardware supply, and end-to-end services — from New Delhi to across India.

Our Story

Building the Future of IT

Stacksol Technologies Private Limited was founded in September 2024 with a clear mission — to bridge the gap between businesses and cutting-edge technology. Promoted by visionary directors Dhruv Sandilya and Pravesh Kumar Pathak, the company was incorporated as a Private Limited Company and has rapidly established its presence in the IT solutions landscape.

Headquartered in New Delhi, we specialize in comprehensive IT solutions, hardware supply, and professional services tailored for enterprises, SMEs, and government organizations. Our focus areas include endpoint computing, cloud infrastructure, cybersecurity, networking, and enterprise software solutions.

As an authorized Government e-Marketplace (GeM) partner, we actively support government procurement initiatives across India. Our GST registration (GSTIN: 07ABNCS9084P1Z6) affirms our commitment to transparency and regulatory compliance in every engagement.
